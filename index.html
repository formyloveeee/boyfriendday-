BOYFRIEND DAY LETTER

For You, Sirr Jii 💌

Read this one slowly.

Dear Sirr Jii,

Happy Boyfriend Day. ♡

Mujhe nahi pata ki main is letter ko exactly kaise start karun, because there are honestly so many things I want to say.

Agar main sirf “I love you” likh doon, toh woh true toh hoga... but it won't explain everything.

It won't explain the happiness of seeing your notification, the comfort of hearing your voice, the random excitement of telling you something stupid, or the way I sometimes wait for your reply without even realising how long I've been waiting.

It won't explain how much I remember the little things.

Aapka “madam jii”. Mera “sirr jii”. Our random conversations. Our silly moments. The caring little questions. The times we laughed. The times things weren't easy either.

Humari story perfect nahi hai. Aur main usse perfect dikhana bhi nahi chahti.

Because real feelings aren't always perfectly organised.

Kabhi main overthink karti hoon. Kabhi chhoti baat zyada feel hoti hai. Kabhi distance difficult lagta hai. Kabhi bas aapki thodi si attention aur reassurance chahiye hoti hai.

But even through all those emotions, one thing has stayed the same:

You matter to me.

More than I probably manage to explain.

I hope you know that I notice your little efforts. I notice your words. I notice the things you casually tell me.

And even when I don't say everything perfectly, please know that there is always a lot of feeling behind my silence.

Today is Boyfriend Day, but honestly, I don't want to celebrate just a label.

I want to celebrate the person behind it.

The person who became a part of my days. The person whose name can change my mood. The person I can call “sirr jii” and somehow feel at home in that little word.

So thank you.

For the memories. For the conversations. For the laughs. For the little moments. For simply being a part of this chapter of my life.

And if you ever wonder what this whole website means...

It means I had too many feelings and nowhere big enough to put them.

So I made you a little world instead.

Happy Boyfriend Day, Sirr Jii. ♡

Please keep taking care of yourself, keep smiling, and never forget that somewhere, someone remembers a lot of little things about you.

Always your
Madam Jii ♡
